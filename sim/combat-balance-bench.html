Calibration — the DEV equivalence table & model assumptions these constants are the balance model · edit and everything recomputes
Value equivalences (DEV)
1 damage = 1.0 is the numéraire. Everything else is priced against it. V_turn (an extra turn) and the value of a point of permanent attack are the squishiest — they set how much tempo and scaling are worth.
Play model
How a turn actually plays out: how many matches you steer, how many skulls you line up, how often cascades and 4+ extra-turns fire. The variance band and death risk fall out of cascade λ and p(4+ extra turn).
Engine constants (from source — read-only)
Spawn weights color 16 · skull 20 · total 96 → P(skull)=0.2083, P(color)=0.1667. Board 8×8=64 → E[skulls on board]=13.3. Matched-skull damage N + max(0, A−1) (attack term is per-match, not per-skull). Destroyed-skull N × (1 + ⌊A/3⌋). Bonus mana ⌊M/3⌋ per color per match. Enemy HP mult [1.15 … 4.75], enemy atk bonus [0,0,0,1,1,1,2,2,2,3]. Level-up +1 atk / +1 mag / +8 HP. HP refreshes full each fight.
A standalone modelling tool. [CODE] formulas (skull damage, scaling, mana, floor curves) are exact to the source; the DEV equivalences and play model are tunable estimates — open Calibration to move them. The power number is √(eDPS·eHP): the combatant with the higher power wins a deterministic attrition fight, and the variance band shows how often luck overturns that.
