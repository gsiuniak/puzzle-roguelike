# Decision #4 — Extra turns are non-cumulative retain-turn flags, ACTION-scoped.

> Extracted verbatim from CLAUDE.md §7 (2026-07-14 split). Historical record — reflects the design at time of writing.

**Extra turns are non-cumulative retain-turn flags, ACTION-scoped.** `_extraTurnEarned` is a boolean. The scene reads `pendingExtraTurn` and `extraTurnTriggerPos` (one-shot cleared via getState). **The flag is set during an action, consumed EXACTLY ONCE at the action's epilogue** — `_finishResolving` (cascade actions) or `_finishInstantAction` (non-cascade casts, part of the single `_castSkill` pipeline — decision #43) — **and is never reset mid-action** (`_beginResolving` deliberately does NOT clear it). This retired the old "an `extra_turn` effect must be authored AFTER `create_tiles`" data-ordering trap: effect order within a skill no longer matters for extra turns (the synthesizer's `EFFECT_RESOLUTION_ORDER` sort is now purely a readability/resolution-order concern). **Enemy extra turns must reset the enemy fire gate.** The enemy turn is timer-driven via `_enemyFired`/`_enemyTimer`; the enemy branches of `_finishInstantAction`/`_finishResolving` re-arm `_enemyFired=false`/`_enemyTimer=0` and stay in ENEMY_TURN — just setting `pendingExtraTurn` (the player's input-driven path) would leave `_enemyFired=true` and **freeze the battle** (the enemy would never act again). **Fresh turns clear stale flags.** Legitimate extra turns continue via the two epilogues, which set PLAYER_TURN/ENEMY_TURN directly and BYPASS `_completeTurnIntro` — so when a normal `TURN_INTRO` completes, any still-set `_extraTurnEarned`/`pendingExtraTurn` is STALE and `_completeTurnIntro` clears both. This enforces the invariant "a fresh (non-extra) turn never inherits a pending extra turn" and prevents a leaked flag from silently swallowing the next turn (the "cast a spell → turn just doesn't pass, with no extra-turn indication" bug).
