# Decision #28 — Boss fights can be prefaced by a video cutscene, and battle backgrounds are data-driven.

> Extracted verbatim from CLAUDE.md §7 (2026-07-14 split). Historical record — reflects the design at time of writing.

**Boss fights can be prefaced by a video cutscene, and battle backgrounds are data-driven.** Two independent, optional per-enemy-def fields drive this:
- **`background`** (asset key) → passed through `battleScene.userData.background` by [`MapScene._transitionToBattle`](../../src/js/scenes/MapScene.js); [`BattleScene.onEnter`](../../src/js/ui/BattleScene.js) sets it as the full-canvas bar-fill image (`setBackgroundImage`), falling back to `battle_background_default` when absent. Register the asset in [`main.js` ASSET_MAP](../../src/js/main.js#L35). (Lord Malakor uses `battle_background_malakor`.)
- **`introVideo`** (URL relative to index.html) → when present, `_transitionToBattle` `configure()`s the singleton [`BossIntroScene`](../../src/js/scenes/BossIntroScene.js) (videoSrc + the enemy's `music.trackKey`/`isSpecialTrack` + `nextScene:'BattleScene'`) and `fadeToScene('BossIntroScene')` INSTEAD of fading straight to the battle. The BattleScene is still constructed/registered first, so when the cutscene ends it just cross-fades into the already-prepared fight. The video is NOT an AssetManager entry — BossIntroScene creates its own `<video>`.
- **Music continuity:** BossIntroScene starts the boss track via `startBattleMusic` (setting `_battleMusicActive`) — **on the video's first paintable frame** (or on the finish fallback), not on entry, so music never plays over a black screen while the video buffers (see the BossIntroScene row). The video is also **preloaded on the map** by `MapScene._maybePreloadBossIntro()` so the first frame is typically ready immediately. BattleScene's `_updateMusicFromState_persistent` → `startBattleMusic(sameKey, true)` is idempotent and the track plays unbroken across the scene fade. (The non-persistent original music path hardcodes `battle_theme` and ignores special tracks — a pre-existing limitation, unaffected here.)
